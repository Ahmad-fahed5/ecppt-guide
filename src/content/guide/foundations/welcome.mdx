## About this platform

This is a hands-on, offensive-security learning platform. Content is organized
into a **knowledge base** of study guides, grouped into sections that follow a
recommended learning order. The [roadmap](/roadmap) shows the full path.

Content here is written from a practical penetration-testing perspective. It is
meant to be used in **authorized labs and assessments only**.

## How the knowledge base is organized

Every guide belongs to a section (for example `Foundations`, `Web Security`, or
`Active Directory`). Sections map to roadmap phases, so you can learn in order
or jump straight to a topic.
